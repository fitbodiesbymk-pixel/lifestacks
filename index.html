The Concept

Life is full. Are you living all of it?

Most of us are good at one or two things. We grind at work, hit the gym, maybe catch up with a friend here and there. But when was the last time you felt genuinely full — like you really showed up in every area of your life on the same day?

LifeStacks is a framework built around the idea that a great life isn't about doing one thing perfectly — it's about consistently showing up across five key pillars that research and experience show matter most. Not perfectly. Not obsessively. Just intentionally, every day.

Think of it like stacking small wins. Each pillar you touch is a win. Stack enough of them and you start to feel the difference — more energy, more connection, more purpose.

The XP System

Earn points for living your life.

Here's where it gets fun. LifeStacks gamifies your day so you can actually see your progress instead of just feeling like you're spinning your wheels. Every pillar you touch earns you XP — experience points, like in a game. But this game is your life.

The real magic? Stacking. When one activity covers multiple pillars at once — watching a documentary with your partner, going for a run with a friend — you earn double XP. Life's best moments usually hit multiple pillars at once. LifeStacks rewards you for noticing that.

Why Intention Setting

Your best days start the night before.

There's a reason elite performers, athletes, and coaches all talk about planning. Research on implementation intentions — the "when, where, and how" of your goals — consistently shows that people who plan ahead are dramatically more likely to follow through.

That's why LifeStacks includes a daily intention-setting section. Not to pressure you into a perfect plan, but to give your brain a roadmap the night before. When you wake up knowing what you want to do, you actually do it. It's that simple. And it earns you an extra +10 XP just for showing up prepared.
